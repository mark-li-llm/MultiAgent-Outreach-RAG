 Full Understanding of Your Plan - Ultrathink MVP

  Your Vision: Create a dead-simple demo for the entrepreneur conference that showcases
  your multi-agent RAG system's business value to investors.

  The Product (What)

  - One-click demo: QR code → Web page → Select persona → See generated email
  - No complexity: No login, no API keys, no waiting
  - Pre-cached results: Use existing generated emails (no real-time processing)
  - Single company focus: Whatever company data you already have processed
  - 3-5 persona options: VP Sales, VP Customer Experience, CFO, etc.

  The Audience (Who)

  - Investors at entrepreneur conference
  - Non-technical - they care about business value, not architecture
  - Time-constrained - need to grab attention in seconds
  - Looking for: Practical AI solutions they can fund/buy

  The Strategy (Why)

  1. Demonstrate capability: "I built this sophisticated multi-agent system"
  2. Show business value: "It generates personalized, compliant outreach emails"
  3. Attract interest: "This could be customized for your portfolio companies"
  4. Start conversations: "Let's discuss how this solves your sales/IR/PR needs"

  The Implementation (How)

  - Frontend: Simple HTML with dropdown + display area
  - Backend: Minimal Flask/FastAPI serving JSON files
  - Hosting: Vercel/Railway/Render (simpler than GCP)
  - Data: Read from outputs/*/email.json files
  - Access: QR code on your phone/business card

  Success Metrics

  - Can someone try it in < 10 seconds?
  - Do they understand the value immediately?
  - Does it spark a conversation about their needs?

  What This IS NOT

  - Not a technical deep-dive
  - Not showing the RAG pipeline
  - Not demonstrating real-time processing
  - Not explaining agents/embeddings/FAISS

  Bottom Line: You're packaging months of complex multi-agent development into a 10-second
  "wow" moment that opens doors to investment and custom development opportunities.
